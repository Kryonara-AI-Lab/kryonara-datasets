# Governance at Kryonara AI Lab

## Mission
To provide the global AI community with datasets of the highest integrity, ensuring that the foundations of future models are built on quality, transparency, and ethical rigor.

## Oversight Committee
Our datasets are reviewed by an internal board consisting of:
- Lead Data Architects
- Ethical AI Researchers
- Domain Specialists (specific to the dataset topic)

## Data Integrity Standards
Every dataset must pass our **Kryonara Quality Gate**:
1. **Uniqueness Check:** No duplicate entries allowed.
2. **Annotation Fidelity:** Minimum 95% inter-annotator agreement.
3. **Format Compliance:** Data must be delivered in optimized, accessible formats (CSV, Parquet, or JSONL).
4. **Documentation Completeness:** All documentation files (Dictionary, Process, Ethics) must be fully authored.

## Conflict of Interest
Kryonara Lab maintains strict independence. All data sources are disclosed, and any potential conflicts are noted in the `COLLECTION_PROCESS.md`.
