# Ethical Considerations - Emotions NLP v2

## Bias Analysis
- **Linguistic Bias:** The dataset is exclusively in English. This limits its applicability to non-English speaking contexts and may not capture emotion-specific linguistic nuances from other languages.
- **Cultural Bias:** The source material is primarily from Western-centric platforms, which may influence how certain emotions are expressed and interpreted.
- **Demographic Representation:** Due to the nature of the sources, there may be an over-representation of certain age groups or socioeconomic backgrounds.

## Known Limitations
- **Sarcasm:** Detecting sarcasm remains a challenge; some sarcastic remarks may be mislabeled as their literal emotional counterparts.
- **Ambiguity:** Some texts may naturally contain multiple conflicting emotions. While this is a multi-label dataset, the most dominant emotion was prioritized during annotation.

## Recommended Use Cases
- Training educational or supportive AI agents.
- Academic research in sentiment analysis.
- Benchmarking NLP models on emotional intelligence tasks.

## Non-Recommended Use Cases
- High-stakes decision making (e.g., legal or medical diagnostics).
- Surveillance or monitoring of individuals without consent.
